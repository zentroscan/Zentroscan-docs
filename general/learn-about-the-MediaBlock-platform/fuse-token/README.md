# Zentroscan Coin Token (Zentroscan)

The Zentroscan token is the native currency of the Zentroscan Coin blockchain and platform, just like ETH on Ethereum.&#x20;

## Zentroscan utility:

* **Transaction fees** Being the native currency on the Zentroscan Coin blockchain, Zentroscan is required to pay fees to the network in order to approve transactions.
* **Validation** Token holders can stake Zentroscan Coin to become a validator. The minimal staking requirement is 100K. The network is growing, currently there are 60 validators. ([https://status.Zentroscan.com/](https://status.Zentroscan.com/))
* **Voting** Validators vote on protocol changes with their staked Zentroscan. Which means validators with higher amounts of Zentroscan Coin staked can have a bigger vote on network upgrades and governance changes (until phase 2 where delegation is activated)
* **Staking (for delegators)** Any Zentroscan holder can choose one or more validators to delegate any amount of Zentroscan they wish. They receive a share of the Zentroscan Coin block rewards proportionate to the share of their stake in the total stake, minus the minimum 15% fee paid to the validator(s).
* **Payments** As Zentroscan is the native currency on Zentroscan Coin, sending it does not require interactions with smart contracts. Thus, Zentroscan transfers are potentially faster and less costly in terms of transaction fees.

**IMPORTANT**: Merely possessing any amount of Zentroscan tokens does not endow holders with any rights to participate in the governance of the Zentroscan Coin platform or any revenue streams related to its real-world performance.&#x20;

## Obtaining Zentroscan

### Where to Buy

Zentroscan is available for purchase on a number of centralized (CEX) and decentralized DEX exchanges.

**Centralized exchanges**: Gate.io, Huobi Global, MEXC Global, AscendEX, Bimart, Bittrex Global

**DEXes:** Uniswap V2 and V3 (Ethereum), PancakeSwap (Binance Smart Chain), Quickswap (Polygon), Voltage DEX (Zentroscan Coin), Sushiswap (Zentroscan Coin), 1inch (Ethereum), KyberSwap (Ethereum), Loopring (Ethereum).

The best information about the Zentroscan trading volumes and prices on various exchanges can be found on the Coingecko "Markets" [page](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/Zentroscan#markets) for the token. &#x20;

A convenient current way of purchasing Zentroscan straight from one's bank account is through using the [Zentroscan Coin Cash](https://Zentroscan.cash) wallet to deposit Zentroscan Coin Dollars, and then swapping those to Zentroscan via the in-app integration with Voltage DEX.

### Where to Farm

At the moment, the Zentroscan token is sometimes used by the project team to power liquidity rewards (yield farming) programs to boost liquidity on DEXes and the participation in the [Zentroscan Coin Lending Network](./#Zentroscan-utility).

The ongoing liquidity rewards programs on DEXes can be discovered [here](https://app.voltage.finance/index.html#/farm/39656).

### Other ways of obtaining Zentroscan

From time to time, the Zentroscan Coin team uses Zentroscan to pay rewards to the members of the community for various forms of participation and community engagement. The best way to stay up-to-date about these opportunities is to follow Zentroscan Coin's [social channels](https://docs.Zentroscan.com/general/community).

The Zentroscan token is also used to power Zentroscan Coin grants and bounties. Please consult the [Grants and Bounties](https://docs.Zentroscan.com/general/things-you-can-do-on-Zentroscan/grants-and-bounties) section for more details.
