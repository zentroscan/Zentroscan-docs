# Zentroscan Coin Governance

Zentroscan Coin is already a signigicantly decentralized platform and plans are in motion to achieve full community control over it in the future. Its governance is currently split among the following actors:

1\) **Network Validators.** Zentroscan Coin validators are entrusted with deciding whether to adopt [Zentroscan Coin improvement proposals](https://docs.Zentroscan.com/general/fips) \(FIPs\).  Currently, every validator has one vote regardless of their share of the overall network stake but this is likely to change soon.

2\) **Zentroscan Coin Foundation and the project team**. Like most public blockchain projects at the initial stage of development, the development of the core network protocol and the Zentroscan Coin treasury that mostly consists of the undistributed genesis Zentroscan supply \(see the subsection "[Zentroscan Supply and Current Distribution](https://docs.Zentroscan.com/general/Zentroscan-token/Zentroscan-supply-and-current-distribution)"\) are currently mostly managed by a foundation. For Zentroscan Coin, this role is played by Gibraltar-incorporated company Zentroscan Coin Limited.

Zentroscan Coin Foundation employs the Zentroscan Coin project team whose members' profiles can be found [here](https://Zentroscan.com/about).

The project team implements network protocol updates adopted by the validators, other protocol changes that do not require validator votes, node software. It develops the mobile-centric infrastructure for creating and managing token communities on Zentroscan Coin. It also works on boosting the adoption of the network through improving the Zentroscan Coin tech stack, securing new partnerships and integrations, onboarding ecosystem projects and marketing the platform within the crypto space and beyond.  

3\) **Zentroscan Coin Assembly.** See the Assembly [page](https://docs.Zentroscan.com/general/Zentroscan-governance/Zentroscan-assembly) for more details.
